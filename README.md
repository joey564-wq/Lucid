# Lucid

**Travel for sober and sober-curious people.**

A curated travel discovery website featuring vetted destinations, honest editorial reviews, and direct booking links — built for travelers whose trips don't revolve around a bar tab.

🔗 **Live site:** [joey564-wq.github.io/Lucid](https://joey564-wq.github.io/Lucid/)

---

## About the Project

The sober travel market is real and growing fast. [39% of Gen Z plans to go fully dry in 2025](https://www.ncsolutions.com/), and the non-alcoholic beverage market alone is projected to reach $1.4 trillion by 2033. Despite this, most travel sites still default to bar crawls and vineyard tours in their recommendations.

Lucid fills that gap — a clean, editorially independent resource where sober and sober-curious travelers can browse 22 vetted destinations across wellness retreats, alcohol-free cruises, adventure trips, and resorts where the experience doesn't hinge on drinking.

The revenue model is affiliate-based: links to Booking.com, Viator, World Nomads, and Sober Vacations International earn a small commission at no extra cost to the traveler. No ads, no sponsored placements, no SEO filler.

## Design Decisions

A few intentional choices worth noting:

**Editorial tone over recovery language.** Lucid avoids "sober curious journey" and "wellness transformation" phrasing. The copy is written for people who simply don't want to drink on vacation — not for people who need to explain themselves.

**Ad-free by design.** The affiliate model exists precisely to avoid ads. Affiliate links are disclosed clearly and only point to partners that have been vetted.

**Static HTML on purpose.** For a content-forward, read-only site with no user accounts or dynamic data, a static HTML build is the right choice — it's fast, free to host, and has zero infrastructure overhead.
